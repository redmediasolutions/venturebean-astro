The 5-Step Coaching Impact Cycle

From Insight to Impact. From Potential to Performance.

  • Understand your context: Identify current challenges, evaluate current competencies, behaviours

Benchmark

Analyse, Action Plan

  • Work through real situations: Apply insights directly to ongoing business decisions

Implement

Sustain

FAQs

What is executive coaching, and who is it for?

Executive coaching helps CXOs, senior leaders, executives, founders, and business owners improve leadership skills, decision-making, and professional performance.

How does leadership coaching improve leadership effectiveness?

Leadership coaching helps leaders strengthen communication, decision-making, self-awareness, and team management to lead more effectively.

What is the difference between executive coaching, leadership coaching, and business coaching?

Executive coaching focuses on individual leadership performance, leadership coaching develops leadership capabilities, while business coaching helps owners and founders overcome business challenges and drive growth.

How do leadership development programs improve organizational performance?

Leadership development programs build stronger leaders, improve team performance, enhance decision-making, and align leadership capabilities with organisational goals.

How can coaching help founders and business owners scale their businesses?

Business coaching helps founders and business owners gain strategic clarity, improve decision-making, strengthen leadership, and build scalable processes for growth.

What results can I expect from executive coaching?

Executive coaching can help improve leadership effectiveness, decision-making, communication, confidence, self-awareness, and stakeholder management.

How do you measure the success of a coaching engagement?

Success is measured through defined goals, behavioural improvements, leadership effectiveness, stakeholder feedback, and progress against agreed development objectives.

What makes VentureBean's coaching approach different?

VentureBean combines personalised executive and leadership coaching with practical business insights, connecting individual development with organisational priorities and measurable outcomes.

How long does an executive coaching engagement usually last?

The duration depends on the individual’s goals and development needs, ranging from focused short-term engagements to longer-term leadership development programs.

How do I get started with VentureBean Coaching?

Start with a conversation to discuss your leadership or business goals and identify the right executive coaching, leadership coaching, or business coaching approach for your needs.
